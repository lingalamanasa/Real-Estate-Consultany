Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers to Common Real Estate Queries

Transparent guidance on fees, title verification, and transaction timelines.

1. Does Stackly charge buyer brokerage on new developer projects?

No. On all primary builder mandates, our advisory services are 100% free for buyers (0% brokerage), as we are compensated directly by developers under structured institutional marketing mandates.

2. What does the 120-Point Title Audit cover?

It includes unbroken 40-year parent document tracing, revenue patta/chitta scrutiny, encumbrance certificates (EC), DTCP/CMDA layout sanctions, and RERA escrow account verification.

3. Can NRIs buy properties in India without travelling?

Yes. NRIs can execute transactions through a registered Power of Attorney (POA) attested at the Indian Consulate in their resident country, with full remote digital documentation.

4. What are the typical rental yields on commercial SCO plots?

Grade-A pre-leased commercial assets typically generate 8.5% to 11.2% net yield per annum with 15% rent escalation every 3 years.
