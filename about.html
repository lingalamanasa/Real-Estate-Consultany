Our Journey

The Evolution of Stackly Realty

Bridging the gap between archaic real estate brokering and modern data-driven consultancy.

Stackly was established in Salem with a singular conviction: individual home buyers and institutional investors deserve the same level of forensic due diligence and financial structuring that Fortune 500 companies receive during cross-border acquisitions.

In an unorganized marketplace dominated by unverified claims, opaque pricing, and developer bias, Stackly built a technology platform combining 40-year registry data, municipal master plans, and direct developer contracts. Today, Stackly manages over 1,420+ vetted assets across Salem, Chennai, Bengaluru, Coimbatore, and Dubai.

Due Diligence

The 120-Point Legal Due Diligence Matrix

How our legal team rigorously stress-tests every asset before recommending it to clients.

1. Title Flow & Partition Deeds

Unbroken 40-year parent chain trace, legal heir release deeds, probate checks, and revenue record scrutiny.

2. Municipal & Master Plan Sanctions

DTCP/CMDA layout approvals, floor space index (FSI) adherence, fire NOC, and environmental impact clearance.

3. RERA Escrow & Bank Mortgages

Verification of 70% project escrow bank account, developer debt covenants, and non-encumbrance certificates.
